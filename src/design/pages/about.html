Our story

It's not about us, it's about you. We believe great software should help people grow their business. Trusted by startups, retailers and established brands, GEMSOFT works alongside founders and teams to plan, design and build the websites, apps and systems they run on.

Started in 2018 with three engineers, we are now a full team of strategists, designers and developers serving clients in 6 countries, combining senior expertise with a clear process to ship work on time and keep it running long after launch.

How we work

Built around clarity, ownership and accountability

No hand-offs between strangers and no surprise invoices. Three promises shape every project we take on.
