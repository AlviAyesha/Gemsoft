What does GEMSOFT Technologies do?

We design, build and support websites, mobile apps and custom software for growing businesses, all with one in-house team.

What services do you offer?

Web development, mobile apps, custom software, UI/UX design, SEO and growth, and cloud hosting with ongoing support.

Do you handle everything in-house?

Yes. Designers, developers and support sit in one team, so there is no outsourcing and no hand-offs between vendors.

How is pricing calculated?

After a free discovery call we send a fixed quote by scope. Any change is priced and approved before work on it starts.

Do you offer monthly support plans?

Yes. Plans cover hosting, backups, security updates, monitoring and a set number of hours for new features each month.

Can you take on large or complex projects?

Yes. Larger builds are split into phases, each with its own demo and sign-off, so risk stays small at every step.

How do I request a quote?

Use "Talk with us" or email us. A project lead replies within one working day to book a short call.
